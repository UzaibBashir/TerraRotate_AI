# Crop catalog and provenance

The initial catalog lists potato, rice, maize, wheat, mustard, and field pea. Families and legume classification are broad botanical categories. Durations and water-use classes are simplified planning categories that vary with cultivar, elevation, planting date, soil, and management. Entries are not Kashmir-specific prescriptions.

Local verification starting points: ICAR Central Potato Research Institute (https://cpri.icar.gov.in/); SKUAST-Kashmir (https://skuastkashmir.ac.in/); ICAR Indian Institute of Maize Research (https://iimr.icar.gov.in/); ICAR Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (https://iiwbr.icar.gov.in/); ICAR-Directorate of Rapeseed-Mustard Research (https://drmr.res.in/); ICAR Indian Institute of Pulses Research (https://iipr.icar.gov.in/). Exact catalog ranges are provisional until supported by locally verified, cultivar-specific citations. Unknown temperature and pH ranges are not filled in.
