# Official Field Shift resource register and allowlist

**Verified against the official Resources tab in the browser on 14 Nov 2026.** Official page: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/adapting-farms-with-nasa-data/?tab=resources . The tab labels this section “NASA Data & Resources”; it includes NASA datasets and non-NASA resources. Provider below is classified from each item’s owner, not only the section heading.

The challenge lists 24 resource cards (22 in NASA Data & Resources and 2 ESA partner resources). They are suggested materials, not 24 mandatory integrations. The challenge requires at least one listed NASA resource and clear source documentation. This register is the allowlist authority for NASA integrations. U.S.-only resources and products needing credentials are not integrated into the global Kashmir demonstration.

## Integration rules

1. NASA connectors are limited to rows 1–6, 14, and 22. Every integration must identify exact product/version, parameters, dates, native resolution, and access route.
2. Current NASA integrations are POWER (row 4) and NASA GIBS visualizations of SMAP, GPM/IMERG, MODIS NDVI, MODIS true color, and VIIRS true color (rows 1–3). POWER values are gridded estimates, not field measurements. GIBS layers are rendered imagery, not extracted measurement values. Numeric vegetation analysis accepts user-supplied values only when the source text identifies a listed NASA/partner product family, version, dates and native resolution; this does not mean an image or granule has been downloaded automatically.
3. Resources marked “not integrated” are candidates, not data used by TerraRotate. Do not list them as used data in submissions.
4. Non-NASA resources must not be described as NASA datasets. Region-limited resources may be offered conditionally when a field is inside their documented coverage, but must not act as global defaults. Soil characteristics remain manually entered unless a future source is explicitly evaluated for that location.
5. Coarse pixels and footprint sampling must not be represented as field-scale observations. Do not substitute a different dataset when an API is unavailable.

## Global-scope interpretation

“Global-project suitable” means the resource can support a location-aware global product where that product has coverage. It does not mean every location/date has data or that a dataset resolves an individual field. Use the coverage and resolution stated for the selected product. For the project, the strongest globally relevant candidates are SMAP, GPM/IMERG, Landsat/MODIS/VIIRS, NASA POWER, SRTM, ECOSTRESS where swaths exist, GLDAS, FAO GAEZ, Sentinel-2 (listed ESA partner), and ERA5 (listed partner resource). GEDI is sampled contextual evidence. NLDAS and the explicitly U.S./North America resources are conditional regional layers, not global defaults.
